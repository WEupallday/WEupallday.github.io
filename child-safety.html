TheFlap — Child Safety Standards

Standards against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) · Last updated: July 2026
Zero tolerance. TheFlap has an absolute, zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE). This content is strictly forbidden, and any account involved in it is permanently removed and reported to the authorities.

1. Our commitment

TheFlap is committed to keeping the app free of any content or conduct that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors. These standards describe the rules everyone agrees to, how we prevent and respond to violations, and how anyone can report a concern. They apply to every user and to all content, including posts ("flaps"), photos, replies, direct messages, profile information, and usernames.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on TheFlap:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form — images, video, drawings, text, or links.
  • Sexualization of minors, including sexual comments, requests, or role-play involving anyone under 18.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to sexually exploit, extort, or lure a minor.
  • Sharing, requesting, or facilitating access to CSAM, on or off the platform.
  • Trafficking, endangerment, or any other abuse or exploitation of a child.

3. How we prevent and detect abuse

  • Clear community rules. Our House Rules and this policy prohibit sexual content and any content that endangers minors, and users agree to them by using the app.
  • In-app reporting. Every post and reply has a "Report" and a "Block" control so any user can flag content or another user in one tap.
  • Moderation. Reports are reviewed by an administrator who can remove content and permanently ban accounts. The admin can delete any content at any time.
  • Fast removal. Content that violates these standards is removed as quickly as possible once identified, and the responsible account is banned.

4. Reporting a concern

If you see content or behavior that may sexualize or endanger a child, report it right away:

  • In the app: tap Report on the post or reply, or Block the user. You can also open Support from the menu to send us a ticket.
  • By email: contact our child-safety point of contact at theflapsupport@gmail.com.

We review CSAE reports as a top priority. Confirmed reports result in immediate content removal, a permanent ban of the account, preservation of relevant evidence, and reporting to the appropriate authorities.

5. Legal compliance and reporting

TheFlap complies with all applicable child-safety laws. When we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse material, we remove it, preserve the relevant information, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the appropriate regional or national authorities, as required by law. We cooperate with law enforcement investigations.

To learn more about the global standards this policy follows, see the WeProtect Global Alliance model national response and Google Play's child safety standards policy.

6. Point of contact

Our designated child-safety point of contact can be reached at theflapsupport@gmail.com and is able to respond to questions about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance.
